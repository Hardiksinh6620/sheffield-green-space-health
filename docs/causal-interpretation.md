# Association, confounding, and interpretation

Area-level health and green-space patterns may both relate to deprivation, age structure, housing, transport, air quality, population density, measurement choices, and other factors. A visual overlay does not isolate these relationships.

Avoid converting area-level patterns into conclusions about individuals. Record uncertainty and spatial scale, and consider whether adjacent areas are statistically independent before applying ordinary tests.

The portfolio should describe the report's findings as reported associations and interpretations. Stronger causal language would require a design and evidence capable of supporting it.
